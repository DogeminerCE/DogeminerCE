# Beta v0.1 is out on https://dogeminerce.com/ !
## This update brings:

**TIME TRAVEL IS FINALLY HERE! :timer:**
The D.I.A. has an assignment for you! Meet Burt, choose your badge, complete the mission, and start your next timeline with Dogediamonds, stronger badges, and Super Fortunes!
Protected Fortunes stay with you, and Statistics now keeps a history of your timelines, earnings, mining time, and mission choices.

**STORIES! :book:**
Added the Earth goal, Moon map, Mars and Jupiter discoveries, D.I.A. contact, Titan progression, and the return stories for later timelines!
The Altar of the SunDoge, Robo-Yoga, Titan blueprints, and Reallyhardium progression are here too. Also, DJ Kittenz can finally release a hit song and earn royalties :)

**DOGEDIAMONDS & BONUS COINS! :diamond: :bonuscoin:**
Find Dogediamonds in rocks, keep them between timelines, and unlock the third SearchDoge slot!
Bonus Coins unlock stronger boosts as you progress, collect nearby coins, and show their remaining boost time above the mine.

**CONVERT YOUR DM2 SAVE! :floppy_disk:**
You can now bring your Dogeminer 2 save into CE! Supports JSON, Base64, and UTF16 .doge saves, with a review before importing and backups of both your original and previous CE save.
Items, earned rolls, protected rewards, helpers, names, planets, stashes, and available timeline history carry across!

**:pick: Pickaxes, Fortunes & Mining Overhaul!**
A HUGE balance pass brings mining, item rolls, helpers, critical hits, and loot progression much closer to Dogeminer 2!
Removed the old per-hit damage cap so strong pickaxes can actually hit hard. Your best critical hit is saved and gets its own feedback!
Added compatible dual wielding, hold-to-mine drills, SunDoge staff fusion, and matching tool movement, layering, and sounds. Bare Paw still does exactly 1 damage :)

**MYSTERY BOX FIXES & EVOLUTION! :question:**
Restored the missing Fortunes, repeats, reward cycle, and cooldown behavior. The Mystery Box can now evolve once you reach the right milestone!

**Your helpers have been busy!**
SearchDoge now finds and saves actual upgrade offers. Upgrade cards show the correct boosted DPS, and helpers visibly change equipment when their upgrade is applied.
Helpers can collect loose coins, and eligible helpers will seek out piles! Hold a buy button for BUY MAX.
After time travel, Auto-Pickup Loot can collect new drops and equip new pickaxes. Away mining also earns 30% of permanent DPS for up to 24 hours!

**HOLIDAY EVENTS! :christmas_tree: :jack_o_lantern: :egg:**
Easter, Halloween, and Christmas now have their scenes, decorations, and event drops, including 10 seasonal pickaxe types and 21 protected Fortune types!
Hunt nine Easter eggs across the five planets, Settings, both inventories, and Upgrades to unlock the golden-egg reward. The proper egg icons are back too!

**:controller: Another BIG Controller & Mobile pass!**
Polished D.I.A. dialogue, badges, warnings, choices, rewards, and inventory controls. Holding a button should no longer accidentally accept the next prompt!
Controller navigation reaches both upgrade currencies and the third SearchDoge slot, follows Phone shop cards, opens the drawer, and skips locked travel routes.

Choose Automatic, Desktop, Tablet, or Phone layout! Phone controls have larger targets, cleaner tabs, and more usable landscape space. Settings dropdowns line up, dialogs properly take focus, and the desktop Supporter button stays visible after time travel.

**Settings, saves & the little things**
Added separate rock-sound muting, Background Audio, Less Effects, a 48 FPS option, and saved story/news/hint preferences.
Polished item details, unread counters, helper names, NewsDoge, backgrounds, buttons, and event notices.
Existing Alpha saves get a balance update with a searchable before/after report and an original-save backup. Earned protected/custom items and Super Fortune bonuses stay intact!
Cloud restore now checks earned progress, waits for reward screens, keeps recovery data, and preserves supporter perks.

**PATCH NOTES that actually behave!**
The in-game notes cover the changes since Alpha v0.055. NEW PATCH NOTES is clearer to click, and after reading it the footer returns to just the version number, without the underline.
