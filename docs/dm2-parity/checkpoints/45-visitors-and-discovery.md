## October 17 review fixes

The user reproduced two Ricks after pulling one from the portal. The automatic handoff left the fading emergence sprite interactive for 300ms; grabbing it could place another Rick and overwrite the only tracked visitor. The fading sprite now rejects input immediately, portal pickup requires ownership of the current visitor, and placement rejects a second live Rick. Reset also removes orphan visitor nodes. Head and tool share one field container for dragging, facing and departure; mouse/touch drag owns the expiry hold and blur releases it.

Use the original DM2 `general/rm/rickdoge_smol` texture, extracted with its transparent source padding (120×110) from `https://dogeminer2.com/play/img/sprites/generalkenobi.webp.json`. Render at source .55 scale; tool uses `(110/150) × .78 × .55`, native sprite dimensions, the source 50%/75% pivot and 10/65 local offsets. Emergence uses its original 68×101 aspect ratio and portal-relative alignment. The clean 512px CE head and old baked-in-tool images remain available as historical assets.

The reported 20-versus-99 bag issue was checked against the real award and save paths: all 99 rolls are granted. A typical first-quality stack gives about 16 items and 83 coin rewards, with coin receipts combined. DM2 also excludes already-owned natural items and applies a 45/25/12-second pickaxe cooldown, so one instantaneous batch normally includes at most one new pickaxe. Preserve those rules. The reward scene now explicitly reports the full opened count and the item/currency breakdown. New regressions verify exact awards, saved totals and single granting for 99 bags, four 99-bag tiers (396), and 150 earned overflow bags.

Follow-up evidence is separate in `scratch/checkpoint-45-followup-qa/`; previous baseline/checkpoint manifests are historical. The complete Alpha v0.055 → Beta v0.1 patch-note task is recorded in `docs/beta-release-checklist.md`. Counts remain 91/11/1; no checkpoint46 work.
