## Helper placement and grouping

Auto placing is enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings. New desktop helpers fly from their shop card to a valid field position near the largest nearby cluster of their type. Mobile retains its helper bar. Disabling auto placement restores manual placement; cancelling refunds the exact purchase cost on the active planet.

Select helpers with the desktop marquee or Shift-click, then hover over one of that type for two seconds. **GROUP** gathers the selected same-type helpers into one sprite. Its scale increases gradually with membership, capped at 1.6 times normal size. Its hover label shows the helper type and count. Hover a group for two seconds to **UNGROUP** it with a burst. Keyboard focus exposes the action immediately, and Enter/Space activates it.

Each individual helper keeps its original ID, name, owned entry, and income. `groupId` is saved on individual placed records; grouping never replaces ownership or combines DPS into another helper. Moving a group moves all members. A cancelled drag restores its original position. Reload reconciliation counts every member, so collapsed sprites do not cause extra helpers to spawn.

Held helpers use DM2's time-based angular and vertical-bob integrator, with 1/120-second substeps, velocity smoothing, bounded angles, faster sprite frames, and a flattened shadow copied from the actual sprite. Pickup rises over 200ms. Release falls under gravity (1,800px/s²), then compresses over 160ms; idle float animations cannot replace these transforms. Shadows anchor to each sprite's alpha footprint without lateral offsets. Dragging and manual placement share that presentation. Core pickup, sway, and landing run for direct player interactions even when the browser reports reduced motion. Pointer events record movement; one animation-frame callback updates each held sprite, avoiding duplicate paints. Scene replacement, imports, resets, and lost pointer focus clean up held timers and transient sprites.

## Cursor and HUD

The outlined assets from the user-supplied Kenney Cursor Pack 1.1 replace the KDE controller cursors. Mouse and controller use the same arrow, pointing hand, open hand, closed hand, and disabled states with matching hotspots. The mouse uses native CSS cursors, so tracking does not depend on game render rate. Hover state changes only when the target changes; repeated movement over one element creates no DOM or cursor-position writes. The controller retains animated press/release on its virtual cursor. The pack's license and future credits entry are in [kenney-cursors.md](../credits/kenney-cursors.md).

Upgrade badges count helper types with another upgrade on the active planet, regardless of current affordability; each planet supplies its tab colors. Badges sit outside the upper-right tab corner above the tab, with unclipped overflow and sufficient stacking order, rather than covering its label. The desktop footer displays the active planet and its owned population, including Doge. The compact footer sits at the left edge of the panel. Mobile omits it. The Mars diamond plate uses the recovered DM2 translucent blue fill. Shop cards lift 1px, brighten subtly, and gain a soft shadow on hover.

## Dogebag opening

Bag quality uses four progression tiers: green, blue, purple, and gold. The natural loot pool advances when its remaining unowned items are exhausted; the quality is frozen on each drop, saved, and retained when a mixed-quality batch opens. Bag quality determines its loot pool rather than guaranteeing every reward has that same item rarity. Golden Moon Fortune (Epic, source ID 10) is now installed, so Moon tier depletion includes that item and all twenty source bag pools have complete template coverage. Field bags and the opening bag use solid tier-colored outlines. Claim-strip icons and loot/claim flights use crisp, solid rarity-colored outlines; selected receipts retain a matching solid border. Currency reveals have no glow. Prefer strokes and borders over glows in future visual changes.

Unopened bags never despawn. Counts are saved per planet and quality and restored as one visible pile per quality. New drops travel out of the rock toward their tier's pile over 800ms with the source expo-out easing and 200ms fade; the badge counts the arrival, while the logical count is saved immediately. Bag positions use the recovered desktop/phone polar layout, scaled and clamped around CE's rock so they stay close and accessible.

The pile uses DM2's left and right back sprites, becoming visible at two and three bags, with the source offsets/rotations and dimming. New layers pop over 320ms; the lower-right white/brown count badge pops over 350ms. Arrivals compress the body and settle elastically over 550ms. Counts at 99 or above use the source's pink full badge and periodic smaller bump. CE retains previously stored totals, including totals above DM2's 99-bag stash limit; this change does not discard earned bags.

Desktop hover raises the pile 7px and grows it 8% over 170ms, with the source's brief wobble. Pointer position leans it up to ±0.11 radians over 260ms across a 55px reach. Leaving settles it over 420ms with the source elastic ease. Phone touches do not start desktop hover. The controller virtual cursor triggers the same hover. Idle pile bobbing uses the source 5px / 2.2s each direction and respects reduced motion.

Clicking a pile opens all visible bags on that planet, preserving each bag's original quality for its roll. The first-use OPEN prompt remains. The first three rewards launch individually at 110ms intervals, followed by growing waves with 35ms spacing inside each wave. Reward sprites arc over 340ms into the bottom receipt strip and bounce on arrival. Flights follow icons as the strip recenters. Combined currency totals count up over 300ms with quadratic easing and retarget on later arrivals. Future slots stay hidden; controls enable after opening finishes. Item review pops from the selected receipt into a rarity tile and adjacent description/stat panel, with LOOT/EQUIP actions. Currency uses an icon and total. Two white backdrops copy actual panel bounds and rounded corners; mobile uses only its visible panel.

Rewards are granted sequentially before the visual burst; earlier ownership and Wow affect later rolls. The batch is saved before its animation. Reviewing or equipping never grants a reward twice. Closing sends item sprites toward their corresponding inventory buttons. Early closing unhides and lays out receipts before measuring claim origins, cancels unplayed opening callbacks, and does not grant rewards again. Claimed coins and diamonds remain withheld from the HUD until the closing currency flights arrive. Refreshing during the animation retains all claimed rewards and clears transient withholding. Cancelling before OPEN restores unopened bags.

Controller input is contained in the reward view: A activates the focused action, B closes, and D-pad/LB/RB cycle actions. Right-stick cursor and RT also work. Held input on entry cannot accidentally confirm. Explicit opening and held-helper feedback remain animated. Reduced-motion preferences suppress ambient bag bobbing and the extra grouping/shop-to-field effects.

## Mystery Box

The Mystery Box now uses the same rounded panel backdrops, bottom receipts, item cards, stat descriptions, solid outlines, two-tone buttons, and inventory claim flights. Its blue/gold box, ready shake, 750ms opening shake, 900ms jump, 1150ms lid change, sharp rays, and 1400/2050ms prize releases retain its identity. The opened box moves aside before review. A solid SVG outline removes soft alpha halos from the existing box art; no image asset is repainted. Sampled effects commit their final frames so rays and stack layers do not snap back.

Normal balancing follows the audited DM2 `qw`/`Uw` rules: ten ordinary openings followed by a double opening; cooldown is 20 minutes plus 30 seconds per cycle step (20:30 after the first opening, up to 25:00, then 20:00 after the bonus). The cycle is saved separately from lifetime opens; older saves infer it from the available count. Current remaining waits are retained, and future openings use source timing. Offline and delayed-frame countdowns use wall time, without subtracting offline time twice. Full/timeline resets clear the cycle and deadline.

Pool selection only uses reached planets, chooses a bag tier with the source rounding, allows duplicate items, appends the seven Mystery-only fortunes, and excludes another Mystery Box. Selection is uniform without Luck/Loot weighting. After a pickaxe the next kind is fortune; after a fortune the next kind has a 40% pickaxe chance. A second reward is forced to fortune only if the first was a pickaxe; kind memory retains the pre-override selection. Both rewards roll against the pre-opening Wow/DPC before either is granted, then both are credited and saved before animation. Close, refresh, and repeated OPEN cannot lose or duplicate rewards. Existing **CE Supporter always-double rewards** remain a deliberate CE perk; they are not claimed as DM2 parity. No ad/bribe flow was added.
