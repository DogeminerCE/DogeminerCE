# Beta v0.1 is out on https://dogeminerce.com/ !
## This update brings:

**TIME TRAVEL IS FINALLY HERE! :timer:**
The D.I.A. has an assignment for you! Meet Burt, choose your badge, complete the mission, and start your next timeline with Dogediamonds, stronger badges, and Super Fortunes!
Protected Fortunes stay with you, and Statistics now keeps a history of your timelines, earnings, mining time, and mission choices.

**STORIES! :book:**
Added the Earth goal, Moon map, Mars and Jupiter discoveries, D.I.A. contact, Titan progression, and the return stories for later timelines!
The Altar of the SunDoge, Robo-Yoga, Titan blueprints, and Reallyhardium progression are here too. Also, DJ Kittenz can finally release a hit song and earn royalties :)

**DOGEDIAMONDS & BONUS COINS! :diamond: :bonuscoin:**
Find Dogediamonds in rocks, keep them between timelines, and unlock the third SearchDoge slot!
Bonus Coins unlock stronger boosts as you progress, collect nearby coins, and show their remaining boost time above the mine.

**CONVERT YOUR DM2 SAVE! :floppy_disk:**
You can now bring your Dogeminer 2 save into CE! Supports JSON, Base64, and UTF16 .doge saves, with a review before importing and backups of both your original and previous CE save.
Items, earned rolls, protected rewards, helpers, names, planets, stashes, and available timeline history carry across!
